# Manuscript render check

`paper/revised/paper_revised.pdf` was rendered to nine letter-size page images
with `pdftoppm` and inspected page by page at original detail. All pages were
present and ordered. No clipped text, broken glyphs, truncated tables,
unreadable axes, duplicate pages, orphan headings, or content outside the page
boundaries was found. Figure captions remained attached to their figures. The
first page has ordinary title-page whitespace; it does not indicate missing
content.

This is a visual rendering check, not a substitute for the automated numerical
and claim-traceability tests.
